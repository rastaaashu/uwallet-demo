Transfer

From quote to verified delivery

Same network or across networks. You see the route, the fee and the maximum debit before you approve with one signature.

How it works

  1. PreparePick the asset, the destination network and the amount. The quote locks fees and the maximum debit for 60 seconds.
  2. ApproveOne wallet signature over exactly what you reviewed. A changed plan is refused.
  3. TrackTwo independent network readers must agree on every chain before the transfer shows as delivered.
